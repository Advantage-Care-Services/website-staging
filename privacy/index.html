Privacy

Last Updated: October 9, 2026

Privacy Policy

Thank you for visiting Advantage Care Services, Inc.'s website at https://advantage-care-services.github.io ("Website"). Your privacy is important to us, and we are committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your data.

01

Information We Collect

We collect the following types of personal information when you fill out a Contact Form, Get Care Now request, or Employment Application on our website. This includes some, or all of the following, depending on which form you fill out and how you fill it out:

  • Name
  • Email Address
  • Phone Number
  • City and ZIP Code
  • Details about the care you are requesting, such as the type of service, county, preferred language, and preferred start date
  • For job applicants: the position you are applying for, availability, experience, certifications, work authorization, transportation, and consent to background screening

We also use Google Analytics to collect data on website traffic and usage. Please refer to Google's Privacy Policy for more information on how they handle data. Additionally, we use tools by Ahrefs Services to collect data on website traffic and usage. Please refer to Ahrefs' Privacy Policy for more information on how they handle data.

02

How We Collect Information

We collect personal information through forms on our website. Additionally, Google Analytics and Ahrefs automatically collect data when you visit our website.

03

Use of Information

We use the collected information for the following purposes:

  • Responding to inquiries and new requests for care
  • Reviewing employment applications
  • Analyzing website traffic and usage through Google Analytics
  • Analyzing website traffic and usage through Ahrefs
04

Sharing of Information

We do not share or sell your personal information with third parties, except as needed to complete the pre-employment background screening that job applicants authorize on our Employment Application.

05

Data Security

We take data security seriously and use encryption. However, please note that our website does not directly store the information you submit. Form submissions are sent directly to our email inbox. Please do not include medical or health information in website forms; our team will collect any information needed to plan your care directly with you.

06

Data Retention

We retain personal information submitted via our website forms in our email inbox for as long as necessary to respond to inquiries, requests, or applications. We do not store copies beyond that.

07

User Rights

You have the right to request the deletion of your personal information. To exercise this right or if you have any privacy-related concerns or requests, please contact us by phone or mail using the contact information below.

08

Additional Terms

This Privacy Policy solely applies to the Advantage Care Services, Inc. website and does not cover any external websites linked to or from our site. Furthermore, it does not cover any services provided through working with Advantage Care Services, Inc., as this policy only pertains to the use of the Website.
