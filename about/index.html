About Advantage Care Services, Inc.

Bringing comfort and care to your home.

From our office in Bolingbrook, Illinois, we provide non-medical home care, homemaker services, and personal care services to families across Will, DuPage, Cook, and nearby counties. Our caregivers offer compassionate, hands-on support with everyday activities, helping clients remain safely and comfortably in their own homes.
