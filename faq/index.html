01 Services

What services do you provide?

We provide non-medical home care, homemaker services, and personal care services. Our homemakers help with activities of daily living according to each client's care plan, including meal planning and preparation, personal hygiene and grooming, transferring and positioning, medication and appointment reminders, light housekeeping, laundry, shopping, errands, and companionship. See all services.

02 Services

Do you provide medical care?

No. We provide non-medical home care, focused on personal care, household support, and help with everyday activities. We can provide medication reminders and accompany clients to doctor appointments and therapy sessions.

03 Service Area

What areas do you serve?

We serve PSA 2, 12, and 13 in Illinois, including Will, DuPage, Cook, Kankakee, Grundy, Kendall, Kane, McHenry, and Lake counties. Our caregivers come directly to clients' homes.

04 Caregivers

How are your caregivers screened?

Our caregivers and homemakers go through pre-employment screening, training, and criminal background checks, and receive ongoing monitoring and supervision. Our team has more than 20 years of combined experience in the home care and home health industry.

05 Coverage

Do you work with state programs or health plans?

Yes. Licensed by the Illinois Department of Human Services (DHS), through the Illinois Department on Aging (IDOA), to provide non-medical home care services under the Home Services Program and an in-home/community care program. We also work with Managed Care Organizations and health plans including Humana, Aetna, Blue Cross Blue Shield, Meridian, Molina, and CountyCare. Contact us to talk about your situation and coverage.

06 Languages

What languages does your team speak?

Our team speaks English, Spanish, French, Polish, Hindi, Mandarin, Russian, German, Hebrew, Lingala, Swahili, and Flemish.

07 Deep Cleaning

What is your deep cleaning service?

We have a dedicated team for homes with extremely poor or unsanitary living conditions. It's an intensive cleanup designed to transform heavily soiled or neglected homes, and it's available in addition to our routine homemaker and personal care support.

08 Getting Started

How do I get started?

Call us at 331-431-3040 or send a care request online. We'll talk with you about the support you need and next steps.
